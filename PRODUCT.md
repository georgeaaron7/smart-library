# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The user specified Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, ZXing, Open Library, and optional Brevo, deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Librarians and teachers operate the application on laptops and phones. Students and parents receive cards or email only, without accounts.

## Product Purpose

Replace manual school-library circulation with a low-typing, camera-assisted workflow and maintain accurate due-date records.

## Operating Context

Scan a student QR card and a unique physical-copy QR label to issue a book; scan the copy to return it. ISBN scanning speeds up cataloging.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Private student data requires librarian-only authentication, row-level security, opaque QR tokens, audit records, and server-only secrets. The MVP avoids paid AI APIs and paid infrastructure.

## Brand Commitments

Indian School Al Ghubra identity, official crest, predominantly white UI, restrained blue and gold, and the footer “Made with ♥ by Julia.”

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied a detailed product requirements document. The crest is from the official school site. No live Supabase project, librarian account, or uploaded image asset was present in the workspace.

## Product Principles

Minimize typing. Keep physical copies distinct from titles. Make AI suggestions explainable. Preserve privacy and reliability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus, labeled controls, contrast, mobile touch targets, and camera manual-entry fallback are required.
